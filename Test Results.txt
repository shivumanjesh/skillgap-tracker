How Each Module Really Operates
1. 👨‍🎓 Student 30-Day Sprint Roadmap & STAR Resume Generator
What it does: Inspects the student's assigned career role and filters out every skill that is already confident. It ranks the remaining skills by deficit priority (not_started $\to$ learning $\to$ comfortable).
Live Proof:
If a student is at 0% readiness, Week 1 automatically locks onto their lowest skill (e.g. Linux System Administration).
If the student updates that skill to Confident, Week 1 automatically shifts to the next lowest skill (CI/CD Pipeline Automation).
If the student achieves 100% readiness (e.g., Karthik Nair), the roadmap automatically switches from a deficit plan to a congratulatory Tier-1 alumni mock interview guide.
The STAR Resume Generator queries the database and only prints resume bullets for competencies marked confident or comfortable.

What it does: Reads the mentee's exact readiness score and branch from the database.

What it does: Queries every student in the database, calculates their live readiness score, and buckets them into the 4 tiers:
Tier-1 Product (15+ LPA): Readiness $\ge 80%$

Tier-3 Services (4–8 LPA): Readiness $40% - 59%$
Remedial Intervention: Readiness $< 40%$
Live Proof: The executive pitch dynamically prints the exact student counts, percentages, and placement-ready ratios of the current database batch. If new students register or improve, the pitch numbers update immediately.

What it does: Calculates the average gap percentage across all students enrolled in each track (AI/ML, Full-Stack, DevOps, Cloud).
